We are very excited to welcome **Bas Dijkstra** as a speaker at Brighton JUG. Bas is a Test automation trainer and
consultant at On Test Automation. He will be giving a talk on - **Solving the integration testing puzzle with contract
testing**

Modern applications that are built out of loosely coupled pose some challenges in terms of integration and E2E testing.
Contract testing is an approach that is meant to address these challenges. In this talk and live demo, I'll

* introduce the concept of contract testing
* discuss different approaches to contract testing
* give a demo of how contract testing in Java works using the Pact framework
* address benefits and implementation challenges of contract testing

will be drinks and pizza courtesy of Runway East and Silicon Brighton, and a chance to chat with the Brighton Kotlin
community for those attending in person.

**Bas Dijkstra**
